Privacy Policy

Applies to applications published by Lizura Games · Effective date: 27 September 2026

This policy describes how Lizura Games (“we”, “us”) handles data in the applications we publish on Google Play. It applies to our apps in general; app-specific details are additionally summarized in the “Data safety” section of each app’s store listing.

Summary

  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We operate no servers and never receive this data.
  • Our apps may show advertising served by Google AdMob. Advertising partners process certain device data to serve and measure ads.
  • Before personalized advertising is shown in the EEA and the UK, we ask for your consent through Google’s consent form. You can decline and still play with non-personalized ads.
  • We do not require registration or accounts, and we do not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Data stored on your device

Our apps keep your game progress, settings, and preferences in local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not uploaded to us (we operate no servers that receive it), and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Our apps may display advertising through the Google AdMob service (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When ads are served, Google and its advertising partners may process data such as: your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location (country or city level, derived from IP), device make, model and operating-system version, and information about your interactions with ads. This data is used to serve ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, detect fraud, and measure ad performance.

Where required by law (including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before serving personalized advertising, using Google’s consent management form. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads instead, which rely on contextual information rather than a profile of your interests. Where our apps provide an option in their settings, you can revisit or change your advertising choices at any time.

In-app purchases

If an app offers optional in-app purchases, they are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Data we do not collect

We do not require account registration, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contact list, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise (GPS) location. Our apps do not include analytics SDKs.

Children

Our apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children below the age of digital consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will address it.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

We process advertising-related data on the basis of your consent (personalized advertising, collected through the consent form) or our legitimate interests (non-personalized advertising and the operation of the app), as applicable.

Your rights and choices

Depending on your country, you have rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection regarding personal data. Because we hold no server-side data about you, the most effective ways to exercise control are: deleting the app (removes all locally stored data), resetting or limiting your device’s advertising identifier in the device settings, and managing ad personalization in your Google account settings. For anything else, contact us by e-mail below.

Third parties

The main third party processing data in our apps is Google (AdMob and Google Play services). You can read Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and Google’s explanation of how AdMob uses data at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our apps evolve. The effective date at the top shows the current version; significant changes will be reflected in the affected apps’ store listings.
